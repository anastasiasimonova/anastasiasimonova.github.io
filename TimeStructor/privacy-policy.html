TimeStructor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. The short version

TimeStructor is built to keep your data on your device.

  • Everything you track — Activities, Groups, intervals, templates, settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not run a server for it and we cannot see it.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers in the App.
  • Cloud sync is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, your data goes to your own cloud storage account — Dropbox or Google Drive, whichever you connect — not to us.
  • We receive personal data from you in only two situations: when you buy a subscription (handled by Google Play and our subscription provider), and when you choose to send us a bug report.

The rest of this policy sets that out in detail, as required by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores on your device: your Activities and Groups, their names, colours, icons, targets and limits, your tracked intervals, export templates, notification settings, and app preferences such as language and time format. It also keeps a locally generated device identifier, used to tell your devices apart when cloud sync is enabled.

This data is not transmitted to us. It lives in the App's private storage on your device.

System backups. The App allows Android's own backup mechanism to include this data: Android Auto Backup copies it to your Google account, if you have backup switched on. That copy is made by the operating system, goes to your account, and is governed by Google's privacy policy — we neither perform it nor have access to it. You can turn it off in your device's system settings.

Deleting the App deletes the data on the device. We cannot restore it.

4. Cloud sync (optional)

Cloud sync is disabled until you connect a storage provider yourself. The providers currently supported are Dropbox and Google Drive. Only one of them is active at a time: connecting one moves sync to it.

When you connect a provider:

  • You sign in with the provider itself: Dropbox opens its own login page in a system browser tab, and Google Drive uses the account chooser provided by Google Play services. We never see or receive your password.
  • The App stores what it needs to stay connected on your device, in the operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore-backed storage). For Dropbox these are the access and refresh tokens. For Google Drive there is no refresh token: Google Play services issues short-lived access tokens, and the App keeps only a marker that you granted access.
  • Your tracking data is written as files into your own account with that provider — the app-specific folder Dropbox creates for TimeStructor, or the hidden application data folder that Google Drive keeps for the App. It is not sent to us, and we have no access to it.
  • On Google Drive the App requests one permission only (drive.appdata). It grants access to that hidden application folder and to nothing else in your Drive: the App cannot see, read, or change your other files.
  • The App reads the e-mail address of the connected account in order to display it on the Cloud sync screen, so you can see which account is in use. It is fetched live, shown partly masked, and is neither stored nor written to logs.

Each provider handles the data in your account on its own responsibility rather than ours, under its own privacy policy (Dropbox: https://www.dropbox.com/privacy, Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can disconnect at any time on the Cloud sync screen, which removes the stored tokens or the access marker from your device. You can also revoke the App's access in your Dropbox account settings, or, for Google, in your Google Account under the apps connected to it (https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

Disconnecting does not delete files that were already uploaded. Delete them in Dropbox itself. For Google Drive, note that the application data folder is hidden from the Drive interface by Google's design — you will not find those files in My Drive or in search; Google lets you erase an app's hidden data from Google Drive settings on the web, in the section listing the apps connected to your Drive.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold by Google through Google Play. Google acts as the seller: it processes your payment, and we never receive your payment card details.

To determine whether your subscription is active, the App uses Adapty, a subscription management service (https://adapty.io). Adapty processes:

  • a subscription profile identifier generated for your installation — we do not pass Adapty any name, e-mail address, or account identifier of yours, so the profile is not linked by us to your identity;
  • technical data about your device and app installation, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and your IP address, from which your store country is determined;
  • purchase and transaction data received from Google, such as which product was bought, when it renews or expires, and whether it was refunded.

Adapty handles this data only on our behalf and on our instructions, under a data processing agreement, and its own privacy policy describes what it does in detail. Google additionally provides us with sales and subscription reports in aggregate form, which do not identify individual users.

6. Diagnostic log and bug reports

The App keeps a local diagnostic log on your device, with a size limit and rotation. It records technical events — errors, sync steps, and similar — and is designed not to record the content of your entries. Values that could carry secrets (authorisation tokens, OAuth parameters, e-mail addresses) are masked automatically before a line is written.

The log stays on your device and is never uploaded automatically.

It leaves your device only if you use Settings → Report a bug. That opens your own e-mail app with the log attached, so you can read the attachment before sending, edit your message, or cancel. Nothing is sent until you press send in your e-mail app.

If you do send it, we receive your e-mail address, whatever you write, and the attached log, at apps.simonova@gmail.com (a Gmail mailbox, processed by Google as our provider). We use it only to investigate the problem you reported, and we delete bug report correspondence once the issue is resolved and no longer needed, and in any case within 24 months.

7. Notifications

Notifications about paused Activities, goals, and limits are generated locally on your device. They are not routed through a server, and no push service receives your data. On Android 13 and later the App asks for notification permission; you can withdraw it at any time in system settings.

8. What the App does not do

  • It contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • It contains no analytics or usage-tracking SDK, and no crash-reporting service.
  • It does not build profiles of you and does not take automated decisions producing legal effects.
  • It does not sell or share personal data, and it does not disclose it to anyone except the providers named in this policy.
  • It does not require an account with us — we do not operate one.

9. Legal bases for processing

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): providing the App and the Premium features you bought, verifying your subscription, and running cloud sync when you have connected it.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): investigating a bug you reported to us, and keeping the App secure and functioning correctly. Our interest is in supporting and maintaining the App, weighed against the very limited data involved and the fact that you decide whether to send it.
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): retaining records where accounting or tax law requires it.

Where processing rests on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time — this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

10. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the App, or until you uninstall the App.
  • The diagnostic log: until it is rotated out by newer entries, or until you clear it.
  • Files in your cloud storage: until you delete them in your storage provider's account (see clause 4 for Google Drive, where the App's folder is hidden from the Drive interface).
  • Data held by Adapty: for the duration of your subscription relationship and the retention period set out in Adapty's own policy.
  • Bug report correspondence: as described in clause 6.

11. International transfers

Our providers (Google, Adapty, and the cloud storage provider you have connected — Dropbox or Google Drive) may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission, or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or by another safeguard permitted under Chapter V of the GDPR.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to request access to your personal data, its rectification or erasure, the restriction of processing, portability of data you provided, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Note that these rights apply to data we actually hold. Data that stays on your device is under your own control, so you exercise those rights directly in the App: view, edit, export (JSON, CSV), and delete your entries, and uninstall the App to remove everything.

To exercise your rights against us, write to apps.simonova@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or place of work.
