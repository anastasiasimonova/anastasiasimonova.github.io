TimeStructor

Time tracker with targets and limits

Make every hour meaningful. TimeStructor is a time tracker with goals: give each Activity a target to reach or a limit to stay within, and follow your progress for today, this week or any period you choose.

What you can do

  • Targets, limits and trackers. Spend at least a set amount of time on an Activity, stay within a limit, or just count the time. A goal repeats every day, week, month, quarter or year, follows a custom period such as every 2 weeks, or is set once.
  • Dashboard and Groups. Start, pause and stop Activities and see their progress in one place. Organize Activities into Groups, each with its own color.
  • Timeline. Every day of the chosen period: total time for the day and a breakdown by Activity. Filter by period and by Group.
  • Activity screen. Progress toward the goal and statistics for any period. A consistency heatmap of the past year shades each day by how much time you spent on the Activity, so you can see at a glance how regularly you come back to it. Add an interval you forgot to track, edit or delete intervals, or move one to another Activity.
  • Notifications when you reach a goal or a limit.
  • Backup. Save all your data to a JSON file and restore it at any time.

The app is available in English and Russian.

Free and Premium

The free plan includes up to 5 Activities, not counting archived ones. Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play: it removes the limit on Activities and adds Cloud sync, export of the Timeline to the clipboard or a CSV file, and two more notifications.

Your data

  • Everything is stored on your device. No sign-up and no ads.
  • Cloud sync (Premium) keeps your devices in sync through your own Dropbox or Google Drive. We don't run a server for your data and can't see it.
  • In Dropbox and Google Drive, TimeStructor works only in its own app folder: it can't see, read or change your other files.

How the app handles data is described in the Privacy Policy.
